# Record to Report

| Step | Key handoff | Common exceptions | AI role (first pass) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Journal entry | Sub-ledgers to general ledger | Manual journals, missing support | Augment review; automate recurring entries |
| Reconciliation | Bank and sub-ledgers to general ledger | Unmatched items, aged breaks | Automate matching; augment break investigation |
| Intercompany | Entity to entity | Mismatched charges, timing differences | Augment |
| Close and consolidation | Entities to group finance | Late submissions, FX differences | Augment close orchestration |
| Accruals and provisions | Business units to finance | Estimates and judgment | Human-led |
| Reporting | Finance to management and regulators | Variance explanations, restatements | Augment variance commentary drafting |

**KPIs:** days to close, share of manual journals, reconciliation exceptions aged over 30 days, post-close adjustments, audit findings.
