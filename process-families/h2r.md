# Hire to Retire

| Step | Key handoff | Common exceptions | AI role (first pass) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workforce planning | Business to HR and finance | Budget changes | Augment scenario modeling |
| Talent acquisition | Hiring manager to recruiting | Role changes, candidate drop-off | Augment screening; human-led selection (bias and fairness risk) |
| Onboarding | Recruiting to HR operations and IT | Missing documents, access delays | Automate collection and provisioning requests |
| Payroll | HR to payroll | Variances, retro changes | Augment variance investigation |
| Performance and learning | Managers to HR | Calibration disputes | Human-led; AI retrieves evidence only |
| Offboarding | Manager to HR, IT, finance | Asset recovery, final settlement | Automate checklist orchestration |

**KPIs:** time to hire, offer acceptance rate, day-one readiness, payroll accuracy, regretted attrition.

Hiring and performance decisions carry fairness and employment-law risk, so they stay human-led even where automation potential is high.
